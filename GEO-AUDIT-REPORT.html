The paradox in one line

You’ve done the hard parts of GEO right — genuinely great, quotable content and near-perfect crawler permissions — yet the site is nearly invisible to AI. Two structural failures cancel almost everything out.

Highest-leverage fix: prerender the 14 routes to static HTML. That single change converts a large body of already-written, highly citable content — and all your per-page schema — from an effective 0 into an ~80, and lifts four of the six categories at once.

What’s working

  • Content quality is excellent (80/100) — real Ads-Manager screenshots, specific CPL/ROI numbers, honest pricing, a full author block. Assessed as clearly human-authored.
  • Permissions are near-perfect — robots.txt explicitly allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot & more.
  • A rich, hand-written llms.txt is currently carrying nearly all of your AI visibility.
  • Security & URLs are pristine — HSTS preload, scoped CSP, clean hyphenated routes (100/100 security).

What’s killing the score

  • Client-side rendering, no SSR. Every page serves an empty <div id="root"> to non-JS AI crawlers — your content & schema are invisible to them.
  • Wrong publish dates hardcoded on 3 of 4 blog posts — a real, cheap-to-fix data bug feeding false freshness signals.
  • Near-zero third-party footprint — no Wikidata, thin LinkedIn, undiscoverable YouTube/Instagram, no Reddit or review sites. AI has no external anchor to trust the entity.

03

Prioritized findings

● Critical — fix now ● High — this week ● Medium — this month ● Low — when possible
C1 · CRITICAL

Client-side rendering hides all body content from AI crawlers

Affects: all 14 routes · vercel.json, dist/index.html

Pure CSR React SPA. Every URL serves the same shell whose <body> is just <div id="root">; content renders from a ~623 KB JS bundle. GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot & CCBot see an empty body on every page.

Fix → Add build-time prerendering of the 14 routes. Fastest: a react-snap postbuild (zero code changes). Durable: migrate rendering to Astro / vite-react-ssg / Next SSG on Vercel. Lifts Technical, Schema, Citability & Platform at once.
C2 · CRITICAL

Per-page title, description, canonical & schema all default to the homepage

Affects: all routes · src/components/SEO.tsx

All per-page SEO is injected client-side via useEffect. To a non-JS crawler, every one of the 14 pages looks like a near-duplicate homepage stub with a homepage canonical.

Fix → Bake per-route title/description/canonical + JSON-LD into static HTML during the C1 prerender. Interim: emit a self-referencing <link rel="canonical"> per route.
C3 · CRITICAL

Wrong datePublished on 3 of 4 blog posts (data bug)

Affects: all blog posts · src/pages/BlogPost.tsx:294

The BlogPosting schema hardcodes "2026-05-24" for every post (real dates: May 4 → Jun 24), and there is no dateModified at all. Freshness is a major GEO signal and this is actively false.

Fix → Add datePublished/dateModified ISO fields per entry in blogPosts.ts and reference them. ~15-minute fix — do it regardless of the rendering work.
C4 · CRITICAL

Near-zero third-party footprint → no AI entity recognition

Affects: brand-wide (off-domain)

No Wikipedia/Wikidata entity, a thin LinkedIn page (typo “appoinments”), undiscoverable YouTube/Instagram, zero Reddit or Clutch/G2/Trustpilot. AI decides if an entity is real from third-party signals — here there are almost none.

Fix → Create a Wikidata item; claim Clutch/G2/Trustpilot, Google Business Profile & Bing Places; rebuild the LinkedIn page; begin authentic founder participation on Reddit.
H1 · HIGH

IndexNow key file is a false positive

/.well-known/indexnow-key.txt returns 200 but serves the SPA shell, not a UUID — so IndexNow submissions fail verification and Bing/Copilot get no instant-index benefit.

Fix → Serve a real static UUID key (text/plain) excluded from the SPA catch-all; wire a publish-time IndexNow ping.
H2 · HIGH

Best-in-class blog content earns zero AI value while JS-gated

The 4 data-rich posts are your strongest citable asset and are 100% invisible to non-JS crawlers.

Fix → Prioritize these 4 routes in the prerender; ensure BlogPosting + FAQPage + author Person render into static HTML.
H3 · HIGH

Unsourced statistics stated as fact

The kitchen post cites “391% conversion boost” and “21× less likely to qualify” with zero attribution. AI engines and rater guidelines discount unsourced hard numbers.

Fix → Add inline attribution + outbound links (Lead Response Management / Harvard-InsideSales). Bonus: creates the missing outbound-citation signal.
H4 · HIGH

Founder Person node is thin and not linked into a graph

Static founder has name only — no jobTitle, image, sameAs, or worksFor — and the four static blocks share no @id, so AI sees four disconnected objects. Author photo uses an unreliable Google Drive URL.

Fix → Enrich the founder Person, add stable @ids linking org ↔ founder ↔ services into one @graph, add an org logo, self-host the image.
H5 · HIGH

Case-study outcomes are unverifiable

“8 confirmed jobs in 30 days” and “21 leads in NYC @ $55” carry no client name, dated proof, or link — the weakest form of authoritativeness.

Fix → Cross-link case studies to the matching named testimonials already on-site; add a redacted dashboard export.
M1–M8 · MEDIUM

Eight month-scope improvements

Undiscoverable social profiles · per-page schema (BlogPosting/FAQ/Breadcrumb) JS-only · no AggregateRating/Review despite real testimonials · no dateModified/refresh cadence · author bio omits “6 yrs / 16 clients” · schema-vs-llms.txt trade-scope drift (roofing/HVAC/plumbing) · llms.txt missing a link section & llms-full.txt · no Reddit/community validation.

L1–L7 · LOW

Seven optimizations when possible

Repeated template across Google Ads posts · thin corpus / no pillar page · free-text priceRange · stale sitemap lastmod · no Content-Signal: directive · CSP 'unsafe-inline' · large PNGs (300–750 KB) → convert to WebP/AVIF with explicit dimensions.
